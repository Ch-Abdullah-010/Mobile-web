Smart Shopping. Smarter Management.

SmartPOS Mobile Store is a demo storefront connected to a complete retail management system — products, stock, orders and payments are all managed from the same place.

Our story

Buying a phone should be simple: clear specifications, honest prices and a store that actually knows what is in stock.

SmartPOS Mobile Store was built as a university portfolio project to demonstrate a realistic e-commerce experience end to end. The storefront lets customers compare devices, choose a variant, check live stock and track orders. Behind it, an administration area handles inventory, point-of-sale transactions, customers, orders, payments and reporting.

Every product, customer and order in this demo is sample data created for the project. No real payments are processed and no real devices are sold.

What makes it different

  • Specifications and variants stored per product, not just a price.
  • Stock levels decrement automatically on every sale, online or in store.
  • Orders tracked through pending, processing, shipped and delivered stages.
  • Admin dashboard with revenue, inventory alerts and category reporting.
  • Responsive, accessible pages that work on mobile and desktop.

Demo notice: products, reviews, customers and orders shown across this static demo are sample data. Prices are illustrative and stock figures are not live.
