Frequently asked questions

A few answers to the questions we receive most often.

Do the products come with a warranty?

Yes. Every device and accessory is covered by the manufacturer's official warranty shown on the product page.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are typically dispatched within one working day and delivered in two to four working days, depending on your city.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, cash on delivery is available nationwide. Card payments are simulated in this demo.

How do I track my order?

Sign in to your account and open the Orders page to see the live fulfilment status of every order.
